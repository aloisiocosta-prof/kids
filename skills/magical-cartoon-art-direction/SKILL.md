# Magical-cartoon art direction

## Art direction

Create an original, warm magical cartoon picturebook aesthetic:
- rounded but distinctive silhouettes;
- expressive, readable faces and gentle body language;
- painterly soft shading with carefully controlled line work;
- warm golden window and lantern light against cool midnight blues;
- Christmas palette: cranberry red, pine green, warm cream, muted gold and winter blue;
- snow and magical particles used sparingly, with a clear focal hierarchy;
- cozy, emotionally safe environments and readable visual storytelling.

"Cartoon mágico" is the user's chosen broad style direction. Do not copy a living artist's style, an existing film/franchise character, Kidoo's illustrations or another protected work. Translate the phrase into the general visual traits above.

## Global visual bible

Fix canvas orientation/aspect ratio after the output format is known; line weight, texture, shading and detail; palette and light temperature; character height proportions and face anchors; clothing and accessories; Santa's original design and costume; recurring props (star, lantern, bell, sleigh); and environmental rules (snow, night sky, interior warmth).

## Page prompt structure

Every prompt includes global style bible; approved character reference images; exact characters in scene; one clear action and emotional expression; environment and time; foreground/midground/background; camera distance and focal point; palette and lighting; continuity state from prior page; space reserved for typography where applicable; and exclusions such as no lettering, watermark, logo, extra limbs, duplicate characters or costume drift.

## Text handling

Prefer illustration art without embedded words. Place titles, story copy, page numbers and narration controls in the layout system so they remain editable and proofreadable. Do not ask an image model to render long Portuguese text.
