# Storyboard of 30+ narrative pages

## Counting rule

Produce at least 30 numbered narrative pages. Cover, title page, dedication, copyright/credits and back cover are extras and do not count toward the 30 narrative pages. Use a manifest with exact page numbers 1..N and verify no gaps or duplicates.

## Per-page schema

For every page create: page number; beat title; narrative text; narrative purpose; emotional beat; scene description; characters present; character actions and expressions; composition and camera; lighting and palette; continuity-in; continuity-out; narration text; accessibility alt text; QA status.

## Initial 30-page arc

Follow docs/book-production/30-page-structure.md as the approved starting structure. It is a scaffold, not final copy. Adapt character details and age-specific language only after the user confirms those facts.

## Pacing and visual variety

- Alternate wide establishing views, medium group scenes and close emotional/action details.
- Avoid repeating the same composition on successive pages.
- Ensure every page changes the story state or deepens the protagonist's understanding.
- Track location, time, weather, costume, held objects and character positions.
- Maintain a legible safe area for text; do not put key faces/objects under text.
- Use spreads only if the final format specifies how they map to individual pages.

## Validation

Automate a manifest check for contiguous page numbers and minimum count. Then manually review every page in sequence. A valid count does not prove narrative quality or visual completion.
