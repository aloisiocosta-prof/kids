# Narration and audio script

## Page alignment

- Every narrated page has one segment mapped to the same page number.
- Narration must match approved final page text; do not introduce plot facts in audio.
- Write natural spoken Brazilian Portuguese, pronounceable names and short clauses.
- Add pause/emphasis cues sparingly for emotion and page turns.
- Keep narrator direction warm, calm and expressive without exaggerated fear.

## Age and accessibility

- Adjust sentence length and pace to the approved age band.
- Keep narration controllable: play, pause, resume and replay.
- Provide visible text alternative and captions/transcript where supported.
- Avoid mandatory sound effects or music; do not make story comprehension depend on hearing.
- Respect reduced-motion and mute settings.

## Research-informed interaction

A 2025 meta-analysis found average comprehension effects of digital-book interactive features close to zero; mini-games may distract, while actions directly aligned with the protagonist's actions may help. A 2023 experiment with children aged 3–5 found visual or auditory enhancements can help, while combining both enhancement types may be less beneficial for younger children. Preserve story flow and use narration as a purposeful layer rather than adding simultaneous competing audio, animations and games.

## Delivery checks

Audio filenames match page numbers; no clipped words, noise, sudden peaks or inconsistent loudness; pronunciation approved; beginning/end and transitions consistent; every segment starts/ends cleanly; transcript and audio synchronized with final text.
