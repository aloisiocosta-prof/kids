# Child safety and editorial review

## Photo permissions and privacy

- Confirm permission to use each person's photograph in the intended private book.
- Treat child photographs and identifiable character assets as private; do not publish them in the public GitHub repository or public CI logs/artifacts.
- Minimize data. Do not request birth dates, addresses, schools or other information not required for the story.
- Do not infer identity, relationships, health, ethnicity, religion or personality from a photograph.
- Document which user-supplied image corresponds to each user-assigned character name and purpose.
- Keep generated character art private unless the user explicitly approves publication.

## Emotional safety: courage

- Do not shame a child for being afraid.
- Do not suggest courage means suppressing fear, never crying or facing danger alone.
- Model naming feelings, requesting help, pausing, listening and choosing a safe action.
- Trusted adults accompany the protagonist during unfamiliar situations.
- Avoid frightening depictions, threats, abandonment, injury or coercive behavior unless appropriate and approved for the age group.
- Santa is kind and respectful; he does not ask the child to keep secrets, go somewhere alone or disobey caregivers.

## Representation

Do not stereotype gender, appearance, disability, family structure, culture or ability. Reflect only traits explicitly confirmed by the user and visible in the provided reference. Give each character a meaningful but age-appropriate role. Keep photo-derived characters recognizable through stable visual anchors without caricature.

## Editorial review

Check grammar, spelling, age-appropriate vocabulary, coherent causality, page-turn logic, accessibility and consistency of names/pronouns. Obtain human approval of the full manuscript and key visual samples before final production.

## Claims

Do not promise therapy, treatment, prevention, guaranteed empathy, self-esteem outcomes or other developmental results. Describe the book as a creative story whose theme is courage.
